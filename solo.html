Questions? Good.

What's in a day?

Six hours of dedicated help, once a week, on the same day each week. Errands, organizing, tidying, schedules, projects and business support. Whatever is on your list.

Why 4, 8 or 12 months?

Good systems take a few weeks to build and pay off over months. A term means your day is held for you and nothing slips back into chaos.

Do you clean?

Not traditional cleaning, so no toilets or showers. I do a general tidy, which I call a "floof": organizing, folding laundry, making beds and clearing surfaces.

How do we start?

With coffee. We talk through what's piling up, pick your day and your term, and I take it from there.
