Good to know

A few thoughtful details.

What comes with my monthly subscription?

Consistent, personalized support from a dedicated assistant. Your arrangement is shaped around your needs and grows more valuable as your assistant learns your routines, preferences, and priorities.

How do we get started?

Send us an inquiry to begin, then schedule a complimentary introductory phone call using the link provided. If we’re a good fit, we’ll arrange a complimentary home walk-through before your subscription begins. Your first service day marks the start of your subscription.

What happens during the first month?

We use the first 30 days to make sure the match feels right on both sides. If it doesn’t, we’ll try to find another suitable assistant. If we can’t, you can end the arrangement without further commitment.

What if my schedule changes or my needs increase?

Let us know as early as you can. We can explore a different recurring day or additional support based on your assistant’s availability. Any extra time is agreed on and quoted before it’s scheduled.

What is the cancellation policy?

After the first month, subscriptions can be canceled with 30 days’ written notice. During the initial 30-day compatibility period, we’ll work to resolve any concerns about the match. If we can’t provide a suitable replacement during that first month, you may end service without continuing the subscription.

Can I reschedule a visit?

Rescheduling isn’t guaranteed, and client-canceled visits don’t reduce your monthly subscription. When your assistant’s availability allows, we’ll accommodate a different time whenever possible.

Does my assistant take time off?

Yes. Assistants take up to two weeks around the year-end holidays and up to two additional weeks throughout the year. These breaks are factored into your monthly rate; dates outside the usual holiday break are shared with at least 30 days’ written notice. If you’d like coverage, we can explore an available assistant and provide a separate quote.

Do you offer cleaning?

We offer a “home floof”: tidying, laundry, organizing, and everyday thoughtful touches that make your space feel more like home. For more specialized needs, we can recommend and coordinate trusted cleaners, repair professionals, and other quality services through our network.
