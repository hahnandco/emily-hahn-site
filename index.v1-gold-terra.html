The fine print, made friendly

Questions? Good.

Do you clean?

Not traditional cleaning, so no toilets or showers. I do a general tidy, which I call a "floof": organizing, folding laundry, making beds and clearing surfaces.

How does booking work?

Silver, Gold and Platinum clients get a standing weekly Google Calendar invite. Everyone else books by text. New clients always start with a coffee date.

What if I need to cancel?

More than a week out is free. Within a week is $45. Under 24 hours is charged as the full shift, unless it's an emergency.

Can I add extra hours?

Yes, when I'm available. Extra time is billed at the standard hourly rate in 15-minute increments.

How do I pay?

I send a weekly invoice through QuickBooks and you pay however you like. Purchases under $200 go on the invoice; for bigger ones you provide the card.

Do you work weekends?

Weekly clients are Monday through Friday. Weekends are possible with notice at weekend rates, though not guaranteed.
